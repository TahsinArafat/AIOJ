## Reporting infringement
If you believe content on AIOJ infringes your copyright, send a notice with:
1. Your contact information
2. Identification of the copyrighted work
3. The URL of the allegedly infringing material
4. A good-faith statement and physical/electronic signature

## Counter-notice
If your material was removed in error, you may send a counter-notice with
the information required by applicable law.

## Repeat infringers
Accounts that repeatedly infringe copyrights may be terminated.
