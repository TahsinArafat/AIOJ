## What we collect

## How we use it
- To provide judging, contests, and community features
- To secure the service (rate limiting, fraud detection)
- To contact you about account and security matters

Essential cookies keep you signed in. Optional analytics cookies are only
used after you accept them via the cookie consent banner.

## Your rights
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access to, correction of,
or deletion of your personal data, and you may export a copy of your data
from account settings.
